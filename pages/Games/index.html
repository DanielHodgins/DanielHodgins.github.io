Tabletop Games and Related Activities

This is an index of various pages on resources, tools, and projects related to tabletop games.



Scrabble

A scoresheet that I designed for competitive play. Use it to record scores and keep track of tiles.



Graph Conquest

Graph Conquest is a graph based strategy game I discovered on social media. I developed an interactive game graph visualizer to explore the possible game states and the moves connecting them. The visualization provides a way to investigate the game's structure and potential strategies.
