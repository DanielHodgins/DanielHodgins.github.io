Choosing a Test for an Infinite Series

A flowchart for determining convergence or divergence of $\displaystyle sum a_n$. 

flowchart TD

    A(["START
Analyze $\displaystyle sum a_n$"])

    A --> B{"Does
$\lim a_n = 0$?"}

    B -->|No| C["DIVERGES
$n$th-Term Test"]
    B -->|Yes| D{"Recognize a
familiar series?"}

    D -->|Geometric| E{"$|r| \lt 1$?"}
    E -->|Yes| F["CONVERGES
$S = a / (1-r)$"]
    E -->|No| G["DIVERGES"]

    D -->|p-Series| H{"$p \gt 1$?"}
    H -->|Yes| I["CONVERGES"]
    H -->|No| J["DIVERGES"]

    D -->|Telescoping| K["Find partial sums
and evaluate $\lim S_n$"]
    K --> K1{"Finite limit?"}
    K1 -->|Yes| K2["CONVERGES
$Sum = \lim S_n$"]
    K1 -->|No| K3["DIVERGES"]

    D -->|No| L{"Are terms
eventually positive?"}

    L -->|Yes| M{"Can you apply
Integral Test? (Continuous, Decreasing, $f(n)=a_n$)"}
    M -->|Yes| N["Evaluate
improper integral"]
    N --> N1{"Integral
converges?"}
    N1 -->|Yes| N2["CONVERGES"]
    N1 -->|No| N3["DIVERGES"]

    M -->|No| O{"Can you compare
with a known series?"}

    O -->|Yes| P["Direct Comparison
or Limit Comparison"]
    O -->|No| Q{"Try Ratio Test
or Root Test"}

    P --> P1["Determine behavior
using comparison"]

    Q --> Q1["Calculate limit $L$"]
    Q1 --> Q2{"What is $L$?"}
    Q2 -->|L < 1| Q3["ABSOLUTELY
CONVERGENT"]
    Q2 -->|L > 1| Q4["DIVERGES"]
    Q2 -->|L = 1| Q5["INCONCLUSIVE
Try another test"]

    L -->|No| R{"Is the series
alternating?"}

    R -->|Yes| S{"Are $b_n$ decreasing
and $\lim b_n = 0$?"}
    S -->|Yes| T["CONVERGES
Alternating Series Test"]
    S -->|No| U["Try another test"]

    R -->|No| U

    U --> V["Investigate absolute
convergence: $\displaystyle\sum |a_n|$"]
    V --> W{"Does $\displaystyle\sum |a_n|$
converge?"}
    W -->|Yes| X["ABSOLUTELY
CONVERGENT"]
    W -->|No| Y["Try another test
on $\displaystyle\sum |a_n|$"]

    Y --> Z{"Does original
series converge?"}
    Z -->|Yes| AA["CONDITIONALLY
CONVERGENT"]
    Z -->|No| AB["DIVERGES"]
